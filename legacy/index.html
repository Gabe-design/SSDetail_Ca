About Us

Detail-obsessed, one vehicle at a time

SS Detail — Showroom Shine — is a small, hands-on detailing operation — no call center, no rotating crew of subcontractors. When you book with us, the person who quotes the job is the same person doing the work on your car.

We started this because we were tired of seeing "detailing" jobs that were really just a rinse and a vacuum. Every package on our menu is priced around the actual vehicle in front of us — a sedan and a 3-row SUV don't take the same time or product, so they're never priced the same, and add-ons like seat and carpet extraction are listed up front instead of sprung on you at pickup.

Whether it's a $70 monthly refresh or a full interior-and-exterior restoration, it gets the same attention we'd give our own vehicles — because it's our name on the work, not a franchise's.
